# When ML Tests Lie: An Atlas of Real Data Leakage

Target length: 7 minutes, 7 slides.

1. **The problem** — leakage makes an evaluation answer an easier question than deployment will ask.
2. **Why an atlas** — tutorials show possibilities; traced cases show how failures actually enter projects.
3. **Bounded corpus** — frozen notebook versions across five Kaggle competition ecosystems.
4. **Evidence method** — establish the boundary, trace the information flow, reproduce when possible, independently review.
5. **What we found** — mechanism counts and evidence strength; populate only after reviewed cases exist.
6. **Before/after cases** — two or three measured corrections, with identical folds and seeds.
7. **Practical checks** — split first, respect time and entities, isolate learned transforms, inspect duplicates; dataset and DOI.

Do not submit claims, charts, or prevalence percentages until the reviewed corpus supports them.
